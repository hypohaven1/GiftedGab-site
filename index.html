About Laysin

Wife and mom of three, so I know tired. I know the 3 AM worries, the mental load, the days when everybody needs something and you've got nothing left. Kids, work, hormones with a mind of their own, or just life being life. These days I'm doing menopause with twin preteens and a teenager under the same roof. Some days I'm not sure who's raising who, haha.

Twice I've been a surrogate for the best families and friends. It was such a great honor to hold space for their biggest moments.

Proud Siberian cat mom here. Through our family business, we share the breed with allergy sufferers who thought a cat was never possible for them.

A lifetime ago, I was a pre-med student, near completion, when a health scare made me rethink everything. I chose to put all of my energy into motherhood. The science brain stuck around, so on your call you get both: the empath who feels it and the mind that thinks it through.

My friends all call me for advice and a good talk. Most of them start with "sorry, I know I always do this," haha. I just tell them to keep calling.

I'm not a therapist or a counselor, and I won't pretend to be. Someone in your corner, honest, open and kind!

House rules

Any conversation that turns abusive toward Laysin, sexual, threatening, hateful, or otherwise inappropriate ends immediately with zero refund and the caller is blocked. Being honest about your own struggles is always welcome here. This rule is about conduct directed at Laysin.

No recording, period. Calls may not be recorded, on voice or video. Recording a call ends the visit on the spot, with no refund, and the caller is blocked.

What the waiver covers

  • You are 18 or older.
  • It is a full liability release.
  • Visits are not medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice.
  • No outcomes are guaranteed.
  • You stay responsible for your own decisions.
